Odoo SaaS operator installed into namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The OdooInstance CRD (cluster-scoped) is now available. Check the operator
is healthy:

  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -f

Provision your first tenant:

  kubectl apply -f examples/odoo-instance-acme.yaml
  kubectl get odooinstance customer-acme -w
