# Choose a support plan and request help

Use the published support terms and provide actionable diagnostics.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Support terms

The configurator lists active support plans, their descriptions, and charges. A free tier can be best-effort; paid tiers can offer different response targets. Use the plan’s published terms for the exact commitment. A response target is not a guaranteed resolution time, uptime SLA, or unlimited development service.

## Prepare a useful support request

1. Include the project, environment, Odoo version, and exact page or operation.
2. Explain expected and actual behavior, steps to reproduce, and the event time with timezone.
3. Attach relevant error output or invoice/transaction references. Remove credentials and unnecessary personal data.
4. Use the support contact published on the website; changing support tiers does not open a ticket automatically.
