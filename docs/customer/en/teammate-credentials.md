# Share or reset teammate credentials

Send temporary credentials by email or download a local file.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Share sign-in details

After creating a new managed login or resetting an eligible password, the credentials dialog offers **Send credentials by email** and **Download credentials**. Email goes to the login address through the platform’s outgoing mail queue. Queued delivery does not guarantee inbox delivery. Download creates a local text file containing the login URL, email, temporary password, and first-login instructions.

> Warning: The temporary password is shown once. Store the file privately and share it through an appropriate channel. The platform does not keep it in browser storage; closing the dialog can require a reset to obtain new credentials.

## Reset an eligible teammate password

The owner can reset a login created and managed exclusively for their team, subject to current ownership checks. A reset issues a new temporary password, invalidates sessions and pending authorized work, and requires password replacement and phone verification again. Existing independent accounts, accounts shared across customers, and accounts with their own projects/billing manage their own passwords.
