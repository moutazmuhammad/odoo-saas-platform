# Navigate your project workspace

Find your projects and select the environment you can access.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Projects and environment selection

The project list and project selector separate **My projects** from **Shared projects**. Owned projects belong to your customer account. Shared projects appear because a teammate role or group grants access. Platform staff can see a broader customer-filtered list according to their platform privileges.

1. Open **Projects**, select a hosting project, and choose a Production, Staging, or Development environment.
2. Use the environment tabs for Overview, Metrics, Databases, Shell, SQL, Logs, or Snapshots. Tabs depend on your permissions and the product type.
3. Use the top project selector to switch projects. The Projects navigation action is also available from Settings and Team & permissions.

## Why a shared project can look empty

A Staging-only grant makes the project discoverable but does not authorize Production. If no Staging environment exists, the workspace explains that no environments are available in your assigned scopes. Ask the owner to create an environment or change your scope only if you need that access.
