# Understand your account credit

Distinguish funded value from expiring system-issued credit.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Credit types

| Type | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Your balance | Customer-funded prepaid value and qualifying returned paid value do not expire. |
| Bonus credit | System-issued credit can expire; use the expiry shown for your account. |

## How credit is used

Eligible purchases automatically apply available wallet credit up to the invoice subtotal. Expiring credit is used before customer-funded value. Invoice cancellation returns linked reserved credit to its original class and expiry. Review the balance, transaction history, and invoice credit line; wallet credit is platform account value, not a promise of cash withdrawal or a bank refund.
