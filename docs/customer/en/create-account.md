# Create an account

Register with your own email and mobile number.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Before you begin

You need an email address, a mobile number, a country and city, and a password of at least 8 characters. Login identifiers and phone numbers are checked against existing accounts, including inactive accounts. A historical contact without a login does not by itself reserve your details.

## Register

1. Open **Get started** or **Sign in → Create an account**.
2. Enter your details and select your country. Check the phone country code before requesting a code.
3. Request the WhatsApp code and enter it on the verification screen. Use the newest code after resending.
4. Continue to the hosting configurator or your dashboard after registration succeeds.

## If registration fails

Review email spelling, the international phone format, country, city, and password length. The duplicate-details message intentionally does not disclose another account. Try password recovery if you may already have registered; otherwise contact support with the error and time, without sending your password or verification code. WhatsApp delivery requires the platform to configure its provider.
