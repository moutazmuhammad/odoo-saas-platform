# View and pay invoices

Read invoice state, line items, balance, and payment results.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Find an invoice

Open Billing using the customer owner account. Select an invoice to view its items, dates, total, and outstanding amount. Use the payment action or PDF download where available. Teammates’ project roles do not expose the owner’s financial records.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Issued and awaiting settlement. |
| Overdue | Unpaid beyond the displayed due date. |
| Partially paid | A remaining balance still needs settlement. |
| Paid | Payment has been recorded and the invoice is settled. |

## Complete payment

Use the available payment provider and return to the invoice or instance page. A provider success screen is not the same as completed platform reconciliation; verify the invoice is Paid and the requested activation is applied. If the provider charged you but the invoice remains open, do not repeatedly pay. Contact support with the invoice and provider transaction reference. Never send full card details.
