# Subscribe to a ready-made service

Understand the managed-service experience and its available tools.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Choose a service

Open **Services**, select an available product, review its description, and configure the offered size and billing options. Products and applications are defined by the platform catalog. Complete checkout and wait for the service to be provisioned.

## Management tools

A service can expose its overview, power controls, resources, billing, and snapshots. Hosting-only tools such as customer Git repositories, the SQL console, Shell, multi-environment workflows, and the hosting Databases page are not part of the managed-service interface. Use the product description to confirm what is included.
