# Launch your first hosting project

Configure a subscription, complete checkout, and initialize your data.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Before you begin

Sign in with a fully set-up account. Choose a compatible Odoo version and edition, a region, and a plan. The quotation and checkout show current prices; this documentation does not fix prices or promise a provisioning duration.

## Configure and launch

1. Open **Hosting**, select a plan or custom size, and enter a project name and available subdomain.
2. Select the Odoo version, region, Workers, storage, billing period, and optional add-ons shown in the configurator.
3. Review the total and complete checkout. Paid provisioning starts after the invoice is settled. Trial behavior depends on availability.
4. Follow the instance status until it is running. Open **Databases** to create a database or restore an existing Odoo backup.
5. Open Odoo from the database row and sign in with that database’s administrator credentials.

## What happens next

Git repositories are connected after purchase in **Code & packages**. Purchased Staging and Development slots reserve capacity; they do not automatically create servers at checkout. An empty hosting site displays the no-database page until a customer database is available.
