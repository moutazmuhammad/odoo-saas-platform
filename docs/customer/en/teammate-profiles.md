# Add and manage teammates

Create a managed login or connect an existing account without taking it over.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Add a teammate

1. As the customer owner, open **Team & permissions → Teammates → Add teammate**.
2. Enter the name and email. A phone is optional at creation; select the country to apply its dialing code and check the final number.
3. Create the profile. A new managed account returns a generated temporary password once. Save or share it before closing the dialog.
4. Assign the required roles separately. Creating a profile alone does not grant project actions.

## Existing and shared accounts

Existing independent accounts keep their passwords and contact details. Accounts managed by another customer require an accepted invitation before association. The owner cannot reset an independent/shared login, or take over an account with its own projects or billing records. Phone uniqueness checks include normalized numbers and inactive logins.

## Delete a profile

Deleting a teammate profile removes that customer’s direct grants, group membership, and invitations. A verified, independently owned, or shared login stays available to its holder and other projects. An unverified account used exclusively by the team can be disabled. Deleting the profile is not the same as deleting the person’s verified account.
