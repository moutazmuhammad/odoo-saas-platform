# Use a free trial

Understand availability, restrictions, and upgrading a trial.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Availability and limits

Trials are offered only when enabled and when an eligible trial plan is available. The duration and eligibility are configured by the platform. Use the expiry date shown in your dashboard. Trial accounts cannot buy daily backups, paid compute upgrades, or additional environment slots before upgrading.

## Continue with a paid plan

1. Open the trial instance and choose **Upgrade plan**.
2. Select an eligible paid plan and billing period, then settle its invoice.
3. Confirm that the paid plan and running state appear before treating the trial as a live service.

> Warning: After expiry the trial is stopped or suspended according to the lifecycle policy. Do not rely on unlimited retention or backups being included; export any important data before expiry.
