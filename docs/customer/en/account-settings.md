# Account settings, language, and sessions

Manage available preferences and interpret time correctly.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Profile and notifications

Settings shows your name, email, company, and phone. The current profile panel directs changes through support rather than offering an editable profile form. A new teammate can change their pending phone during required verification. Account notifications use the configured email; granular notification-channel preferences are not currently provided.

## Language and appearance

Use the header language switch for English or Arabic and the appearance toggle for light or dark mode. Switching language keeps the page destination. Arabic content uses right-to-left reading, while header/footer positions remain fixed and logs, code, Terminal, and technical identifiers keep left-to-right formatting. Product names and user-entered values are not translated.

## Dates and timezone

Dashboard date/time formatting follows the selected language and your browser’s local timezone. Date-only invoice fields remain calendar dates. Raw application logs can show the server’s timezone and are not rewritten into local time. Check the device timezone and include it when reporting a timestamp mismatch.

## Session behavior

Sign out ends access from the current session. Password reset, revoked access, or account setup changes can invalidate sessions or actions. Browser cookie restrictions, private browsing, cleared storage, or platform restarts can affect persistence. There is no customer setting for an unlimited login lifetime. If you are repeatedly signed out, note the timing, device, and browser and contact support.
