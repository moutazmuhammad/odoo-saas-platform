# Complete your first teammate login

Replace the temporary password, then verify or change your mobile number.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Required setup

1. Sign in using the email and temporary password supplied by the owner.
2. Choose your own password: at least 12 characters and different from the temporary password.
3. Confirm the supplied phone or enter your own number with the correct country code. The number must not belong to another login.
4. Request the WhatsApp code, enter the newest code, and complete verification before opening projects.

## Setup is required on every route

Refreshing, opening a shared project URL, or changing browser tabs does not bypass setup. Project and workspace actions remain blocked until both steps finish. Teammate codes expire after 10 minutes and allow 5 incorrect guesses; resending replaces the old challenge. Delivery and verification are also rate limited.

## Testing and unavailable WhatsApp

If the platform explicitly enables testing and WhatsApp is unconfigured, the setup screen can show a test code. You must still enter it; it is not a skip button. This is not normal production phone verification. Once real WhatsApp is configured or testing is disabled, test codes stop being accepted and test-verified teammates may need real verification. If no code or delivery method is available, contact support.
