# Monthly and yearly billing

Review subscription cycles and separately billed add-ons.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Billing periods

Monthly billing charges one cycle at a time. Yearly billing is paid upfront for the annual cycle; any saving is displayed in the configurator and can vary with the active catalog. The checkout total is the authoritative quote, including applicable add-ons and invoice treatment. Changing between periods uses the plan-change workflow.

## Different renewal schedules

Environment slots and other recurring resources are associated with the Production subscription. Daily backups have a monthly renewal even if the main subscription is yearly, and their price follows measured data. Review each due date and auto-renew setting rather than assuming every charge happens on the same date. Stopping the application does not cancel these charges.
