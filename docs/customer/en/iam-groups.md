# Manage team groups

Assign access to a group and maintain its membership.

Reviewed: 2026-10-05

## Create and use a group

1. As the owner, open the Groups tab in Team & permissions and create a named group.
2. Add established teammates as members. Profiles or accepted invitations make users available to the team.
3. Grant the group fixed roles on selected projects and environments, just as you would for an individual.
4. Review membership regularly. Removing a member removes group-derived access, but their direct roles or membership in another group can still authorize actions.

## Management and deletion

Only the customer owner manages group membership. Project Access Administrators can grant project roles to eligible groups within their delegation limits; they cannot manage the customer’s whole team. Deleting a group deactivates it and revokes its effective group access without deleting member logins.
